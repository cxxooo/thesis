Palm Tree Pet — how to open (test version)

1. Double-click "Palm Tree Pet.zip" (in Downloads) to unzip it.
2. Drag "Palm Tree Pet" into your Applications folder.
3. Double-click it. macOS will say it "cannot be opened" / "can't check it for malicious software",
   because this is a student test app that isn't from the App Store. Click "Done" (not "Move to Trash").
4. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to
   "Palm Tree Pet was blocked…". Enter your password, then click "Open Anyway" again.
   (Older macOS: right-click the app → Open → Open.)
5. A palm tree appears at the bottom-right of your screen, with a 🌴 icon in the menu bar.
   You only need to do steps 3–4 the first time.

Click the brown old leaves to trim them. Drag the trunk to move the tree.
Quit: 🌴 in the menu bar → Quit.

This test version records how you use the palm tree (how long it's open, moves, trims, settings,
menu clicks) for Cici's thesis research. It doesn't see your screen, files, or typing.
Turn it off anytime: 🌴 → Test data → Share test data.

Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4). Check:  → About This Mac → Chip.
